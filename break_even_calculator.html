How to read it

A break-even figure is only useful when the costs behind it are realistic.

The calculator treats hotel costs in three practical layers: fixed operating costs, variable cost per occupied room, and percentage-based distribution/payment costs.

  • Fixed costs continue even when occupancy is low - salaried payroll, rent, software, insurance and the base load of utilities.
  • Variable room costs rise when a room is occupied - housekeeping, laundry, amenities, additional utilities and included breakfast.
  • Commission costs rise with the room rate, which is why they are calculated as a percentage of room revenue rather than a flat amount per occupied room.

The core break-even formula is: fixed costs divided by contribution per occupied room. Contribution per room is ADR after the modelled commission, plus ancillary revenue, minus the variable room cost.

Important: this is a management planning model, not accounting or tax advice. Hotels with large F&B, spa, events, lease structures or highly variable labour may need a more detailed departmental model.
