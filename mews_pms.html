Where it may fit

Start with the operating model.

No platform is automatically right for every property. These are useful signals, not a buying verdict.

  • Hotels prioritising automation and reduced manual work
  • Properties comfortable with a modern cloud-first workflow
  • Teams that value an integrated guest and payment journey

Plutus perspective

Software should remove friction from the guest and team journey while preserving the data and controls revenue management needs.
This is an independent evaluation framework, not a vendor ranking. Confirm current features, integrations and terms directly with the vendor.

Demo checklist

Questions worth asking.

Ask the vendor to demonstrate these workflows using a setup close to your real hotel.

  1. Which functions are included in the quoted package?
  2. Can your room types, products, taxes, policies and rate structure be reproduced cleanly?
  3. How do essential integrations behave when a connection fails?
  4. Which reports and transaction-level data can be exported?
  5. How are migration, training and opening-day validation handled?
