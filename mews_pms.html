My revenue-management view

01

Reservation and rate information sits close to the operating workflow, which can reduce the distance between what happened and what the revenue manager sees.

02

The ecosystem is broad, which can simplify a hotel stack - but the property should still decide which functions it wants native and which specialist tools are worth keeping.

03

For commercial work, reporting structure, rate logic, product configuration and integration quality matter more than how modern the PMS interface looks.

Questions I would ask before choosing it

How complex are the room types, rate products and group workflows? Which current systems could Mews genuinely replace? Which integrations are business-critical? How will reporting and accounting map to the existing process? And how much automation does the hotel team actually want?
