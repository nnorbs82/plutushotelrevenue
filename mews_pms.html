My revenue-management view

Where Mews becomes interesting commercially.

01Reservation and rate information sits close to the operational workflow, reducing the distance between what happened and what the revenue manager sees.

02The ecosystem is broad, which can simplify a stack - but a hotel should still decide which functions it wants native and which specialist tools are worth keeping.

03For revenue teams, reporting structure, rate logic, product configuration and integration quality matter more than the aesthetics of the PMS.

Questions I would ask before choosing it.

  1. How complex are your room types, rate products and group workflows?
  2. Which current systems could Mews genuinely replace?
  3. Which integrations are business-critical?
  4. How will reporting and accounting map to your existing processes?
  5. How much automation does the team actually want?
