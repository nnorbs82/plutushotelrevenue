Mews.

A modern PMS conversation built around automation, guest flow and operational redesign - not simply replacing the old front-desk screen with a newer one.

The Mews question

How much of the hotel workflow should the PMS automate?

I think Mews is most interesting when a hotel is willing to rethink process, not just migrate data. The value comes from how workflows, payments, guest journey and integrations are configured together.
