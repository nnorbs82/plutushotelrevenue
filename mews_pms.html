Mews

A hospitality operating system where reservations, payments, operations and an expanding commercial layer increasingly live inside one ecosystem.

THE OPERATING SYSTEM

More of the hotel is moving toward one core.

Mews is interesting less as a conventional PMS and more as an attempt to pull a growing part of the stack into one operating environment. The commercial question is not simply whether each module exists, but whether bringing them together removes real friction.

WHAT THE PLATFORM NOW INCLUDES

The 2026 Mews story is consolidation.

PMS + OPERATIONS

Reservations, room moves and housekeeping remain the operating foundation.

The daily hotel still starts with inventory and operational workflow.

REVENUE

Native RMS

Mews has moved automated dynamic pricing into the platform rather than relying only on external RMS integrations.

DISTRIBUTION

Channel Manager

Powered by SiteMinder and positioned as a native distribution layer connecting to 400+ OTAs.

FINANCE

Payments, accounting and receivables

2026 expansion includes accounting, accounts receivable, recurring payments and broader financial tooling.

INTELLIGENCE

BI + messaging

Native BI and guest messaging pull more operational and guest data into the same environment.

ECOSYSTEM

1,000+ integrations

The platform can consolidate more functions without requiring a hotel to abandon specialist tools where they still add value.

MY HOTEL FIT TEST

Where I would push the demo beyond the polished path.

STRONG FIT

Automation-minded hotels

Properties wanting cloud-native operations and the possibility of consolidating payments, RMS, messaging, BI or distribution should explore Mews deeply.

WATCH CLOSELY

Complex real-world exceptions

Groups, accounting, room moves, unusual inventory and multi-property workflows need to be tested with the hotel's actual scenarios.

COMMERCIAL TEST

What can really be retired?

Compare the total platform cost and implementation effort against the specialist tools and manual processes that can genuinely disappear.
