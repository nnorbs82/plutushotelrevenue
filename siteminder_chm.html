Hotel distribution

SiteMinder

SiteMinder is a hotel distribution platform used to connect accommodation inventory and rates with online travel agencies and other booking channels.

Discuss your hotel setup

Technology is useful when the commercial workflow around it is clear.

Plutus approaches hotel systems from the operator and revenue-manager perspective: how quickly information can be understood, how rates and inventory flow, how teams use the system day to day, and whether the setup supports good decisions.

Commercial considerations

  • Rate and room mapping consistency across channels
  • Restriction and availability flow between PMS, channel manager and OTA
  • Promotion strategy and the real acquisition cost of channel business
  • Direct-booking and third-party distribution balance

This page is practical Plutus commentary, not an endorsement or official documentation from SiteMinder.
