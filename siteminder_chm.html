Connectivity is still the core proposition.

SiteMinder is fundamentally about moving rates, availability and restrictions from the hotel into a wide distribution landscape and returning reservations without the property manually maintaining each channel.

That role becomes more important as a hotel's distribution grows more complex. The commercial value comes from controlling the channel mix while reducing the manual risk of keeping inventory aligned.

My revenue-management view

01

Pooled inventory and real-time updates reduce the manual risk of keeping multiple channels aligned, particularly for hotels with broad distribution.

02

Yield rules, stop sells and channel controls are useful only when the hotel is clear about when different channels should behave differently.

03

Channel performance needs to be read together with commission and promotion cost. Gross bookings can overstate the commercial value of a channel.

04

SiteMinder's wider direction increasingly reaches beyond conventional channel management into booking engine, payments, business insights and AI-era distribution.

THE QUESTION I WOULD ASK

How complex is the hotel's distribution landscape?

If the property sells through many demand sources, distribution control is not only a connectivity question - it is a revenue-management question. The hotel needs to know which channels deserve inventory, what they cost and when their role should change.
