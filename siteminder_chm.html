My revenue-management view

01

Pooled inventory and real-time updates reduce the manual risk of keeping multiple channels aligned, especially when a hotel has more complex distribution.

02

Yield rules, stop sells and channel controls are commercially useful only when the hotel is clear about when different channels should behave differently.

03

Channel performance should be read together with commission and promotion cost. A channel can look strong in gross bookings and much weaker in net contribution.

04

SiteMinder's newer direction extends beyond conventional channel management into booking engine, payments, business insights and AI-era distribution.
