Connectivity is the starting point. Commercial control is the real value.

SiteMinder is fundamentally about synchronising rates, availability and restrictions across a wide distribution landscape. That becomes more commercially important as the channel mix gets more complex and the hotel needs to control where inventory is available, at what rate and at what acquisition cost.

Distribution technology is not only infrastructure. It determines which demand sources the hotel can reach and how reliably the strategy reaches those channels.

COMMERCIAL READ

Four things beyond connectivity.

01

Pooled inventory and real-time updates reduce manual risk, but the hotel still needs a clear strategy for which channels deserve inventory and when.

02

Yield rules, stop sells and channel controls only create value when they reflect a commercial decision rather than static configuration.

03

Channel performance should be compared on net value after commission and promotions, not on gross reservation volume alone.

04

As SiteMinder expands into booking engine, payments, insights and AI-era distribution, hotels should decide which additional functions improve the existing stack.

THE QUESTION I WOULD ASK

How complex is the hotel's distribution landscape?

If the property sells through many demand sources, channel management becomes a revenue-management question. The hotel needs to know which channels deserve inventory, what they cost, when their role should change and whether the technology gives enough control to execute that strategy.
