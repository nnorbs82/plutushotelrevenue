Outsourced hotel revenue management

Commercial thinking, every day.

I handle the decisions that connect rate, demand, forecast, inventory and distribution, with a structure that stays close to the property instead of becoming another distant reporting layer.

01

Dynamic pricing

Pricing should move with demand, not with a fixed calendar or a habit. I review pickup, remaining inventory, room-type demand, event pressure, day-of-week patterns, booking windows and competitor positioning to decide where the hotel should sit.

The goal is not simply to charge more.It is to protect value when demand is strong and create the right reason to book when demand is weaker, without permanently training the market to expect a discount.

That can include room-type differentials, restrictions, minimum stays, package positioning and promotion controls - depending on the property and the demand pattern.

02

Demand forecasting

A forecast is useful when it changes a decision. I track where occupancy, ADR and rooms revenue are likely to finish, how the current pace compares with the same point last year or with budget, and which dates are moving differently from expectation.

Forecasting creates time.It reveals the strong dates early enough to protect them, and the weak periods early enough to do something more intelligent than a last-minute blanket promotion.

The view can be daily, weekly and monthly, with commentary around the drivers rather than only a final number.

03

Channel management

Distribution is not only about being visible. Every channel has a different cost, audience, merchandising logic and ability to create incremental demand. I look at the mix between direct, OTA, corporate, wholesale and other sources, together with the commercial conditions attached to each one.

Net value matters.A promotion can increase bookings and still weaken the result once commission, stackable discounts, breakfast allocation or displacement are considered.

I review rate plans, parity, promotions, channel availability, mapping and the role each source should play for the hotel.

04

Performance analysis

I use pickup, pace, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, rooms revenue, business mix and booking-window movement to explain what is changing. The purpose is not to create a bigger report; it is to make the next decision clearer.

A result needs context.Being ahead of last year can still be weak if the market grew faster. Higher occupancy can still be poor if too much rate was sacrificed. Strong ADR can still hide empty rooms on dates that needed earlier action.

Reporting should end with priorities - which dates to protect, which dates to stimulate, which channels to review and what needs to be watched next.

Outsourced does not have to mean distant.

I work as the revenue function, without pretending there is a team behind Plutus.
