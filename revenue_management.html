Common questions

What hotels usually want to know first.

A first conversation can be exploratory. I do not expect a property to know the final scope before the first call.

Which hotels can benefit from outsourced revenue management?

Independent hotels, small hotel groups and accommodation businesses that need experienced revenue support but do not need a full-time in-house revenue manager.

Do you make decisions without the hotel?

No. I agree the decision boundaries and responsibilities with the hotel. Some actions can be delegated to me, while others may require ownership or operational input.

How quickly can performance improve?

Some corrections can influence performance quickly, while structural improvements take longer. Timing depends on demand, booking window, existing setup and starting position. I do not promise a guaranteed outcome.

Can you work with the current PMS and channel manager?

Usually, yes. I review the existing setup first and only recommend a system change when the likely benefit justifies the cost and disruption.
