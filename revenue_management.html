03

Distribution

Revenue after cost is what matters.

I look at OTA promotions, direct positioning, rate-plan structure and availability through the lens of both demand and cost of sale. More bookings are not automatically better bookings.

04

Business mix

Protect the right demand.

Transient, corporate and group business compete for the same room inventory. The right decision depends on displacement, rate, lead time, cancellation risk and the hotel's wider commercial priorities.

05

Performance analysis

Explain what happened. Then act.

I use reporting to answer three questions: what moved, why it moved and what should change next. Occupancy, ADR and RevPAR are the starting point - not the conclusion.
