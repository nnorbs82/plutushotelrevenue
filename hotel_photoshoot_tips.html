FIELD NOTE 01 / PHOTOGRAPHY

The room has
five seconds.

Hotel photography is part of the product description. It needs to explain the room, create desire and remove uncertainty at the same time.

THE SHOT LIST

Build coverage before beauty.

  1. 01Every room category

    Guests should never have to guess whether the photograph belongs to the room they are booking.

  2. 02The full room first

    Show space, bed position, windows and circulation before moving into details.

  3. 03Bathroom clearly

    Show shower vs bath, vanity and layout. Functional information still sells.

  4. 04Distinctive feature

    View, terrace, kitchenette, sofa, desk, ceiling height - whatever makes the category different.

  5. 05Property context

    Entrance, breakfast, pool, lobby and the spaces that complete the stay.

Before the photographer arrives

Prepare the product, not just the camera.

  • Remove operational clutter and duplicated amenities.
  • Steam linen and align curtains, chairs, lamps and cushions.
  • Test every bulb and keep colour temperature consistent.
  • Clean windows, mirrors, chrome and shower glass.
  • Decide which room represents every bookable category.
  • Plan exterior timing around the direction of the light.
