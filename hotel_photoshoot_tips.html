Do / avoid

Do make the room understandable.

  • Show the full layout before close details.
  • Photograph each sellable room category accurately.
  • Keep the sequence useful on mobile and OTA galleries.
  • Show relevant limitations honestly.

Avoid visual ambiguity.

  • Do not mix room types in one gallery.
  • Do not lead with decorative close-ups.
  • Do not over-process windows and skies.
  • Do not hide the bathroom or layout.

Prepare before the photographer arrives.

  • Complete maintenance and deep cleaning.
  • Align curtains, cushions and lamps.
  • Remove cables, bins and operational clutter.
  • Plan the time of day for each space.

Do not try to fix everything later.

  • Retouching cannot replace staging.
  • Artificially enlarged rooms damage trust.
  • Wrong colour temperature makes rooms feel cheap.
  • Near-duplicate images add volume, not value.
