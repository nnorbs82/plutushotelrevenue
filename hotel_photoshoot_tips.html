5 practical prioritiesUse this as a briefing checklist with your team or supplier.Ask Plutus a question
01

Define the booking story

Decide what the guest should understand in the first five images: room quality, space, location or view, bathroom standard and one memorable feature.

  • Create a priority order by room type
  • Identify the strongest reason to choose each category
  • Plan hero images for desktop and mobile crops
02

Prepare the property

Photography magnifies operational details. Complete maintenance, deep cleaning and styling before the photographer arrives.

  • Remove cables, bins and operational clutter
  • Steam linen and check lamp temperatures
  • Set curtains, cushions and amenities consistently
03

Build a complete shot list

Every sellable room type needs enough coverage to remove uncertainty and show the layout honestly.

  • Room overview from useful corners
  • Bed, seating, desk, storage and entrance
  • Bathroom, view, balcony and distinctive features
04

Use light deliberately

Plan each space for the time its natural light is strongest while keeping exterior views believable.

  • Control mixed colour temperatures
  • Retain a natural exterior view
  • Avoid unrealistic over-processing
05

Select for conversion

Lead with the strongest truthful image, avoid near-duplicates and keep the visual sequence coherent.

  • Match images to exact room categories
  • Export responsive WebP versions
  • Review crops on mobile and OTA layouts

The commercial test

Does this make the property easier to understand and easier to book?

That question keeps creative and marketing decisions connected to the guest journey and the hotel's revenue objective.
