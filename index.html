01 / THE COMMERCIAL READ

Different signals.
One commercial picture.

A hotel can be ahead on occupancy while weakening rate. One room type can accelerate while another stalls. Pickup can look healthy while an expensive channel takes too much of the value.

Revenue management is useful when those movements are read together and turned into a practical next move - protect, stimulate, hold, open, close or re-position.
