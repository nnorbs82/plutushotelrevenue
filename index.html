01 / THE COMMERCIAL VIEW

A hotel is a live market.
The rate is only one signal.

Room type, booking pace, remaining inventory, market pressure, channel cost and guest behaviour are moving together. Revenue management becomes useful when those signals are read as one system rather than as separate reports.

The objective is not to chase occupancy or ADR in isolation. It is to know what the hotel should do next - where to price, when to protect, what to open, which demand to stimulate and where value is leaking away.
