01 / BUSINESS MIX

Choose the business that belongs in the hotel.

Direct, OTA, corporate, groups, wholesale and other demand do not have the same economics. Mix is about value, displacement, cancellation behaviour and what the property can serve well.

02 / PRICING

Price the inventory that is still left to sell.

Rate, room-type differentials, restrictions and length of stay should move with pressure, pace and remaining inventory rather than habit.

Explore pricing
03 / FORECASTING

Forecasting is useful because it creates time - time to protect strong dates, stimulate weak ones and challenge the assumptions behind the budget.
