DIGITAL MARKETING / COMMERCIAL DEMAND

The click is
not the result.

Hotel marketing should create or capture demand at a cost the hotel understands. Reach, clicks and engagement are signals. The commercial result is what remains after conversion, cancellation, acquisition cost, channel cost and displacement.

THE DEMAND PIPELINE

Attract. Convert. Measure.

Seven marketing disciplines make more sense when they are grouped by the job they perform rather than treated as seven separate channels.

01 / ATTRACT
DISCOVERY

Be findable.

SEO, local relevance and paid search should capture demand the hotel understands, not chase cheap clicks.

SOCIAL + CONTENT

Stay recognisable.

Rooms, people, food, local life and events create a more ownable brand than generic destination content.

02 / CONVERT
COMMUNICATION

Change the message when the audience changes.

Email, pre-arrival and repeat-guest communication should reflect different needs rather than send the same promotion to everyone.

PROMOTIONS

Do not let the discount become the product.

Promotions should solve a defined problem, for a defined period, with a metric that decides whether they stay.

03 / MEASURE
ACQUISITION COST

Follow the booking beyond the click.

Conversion, cancellation, rate, commission avoided and campaign cost determine whether paid direct demand is actually valuable.

ANALYTICS

Every metric needs an action.

Traffic, booking-engine exits and campaign performance are useful only when they change a decision or allocation of spend.

THE COMMERCIAL TEST

Marketing is finished when the hotel can see the value that came back.

Reach and engagement are diagnostics. The stronger hotel question is what the activity contributed to rooms revenue, at what acquisition cost, with what cancellation behaviour, and whether that demand was genuinely incremental.
