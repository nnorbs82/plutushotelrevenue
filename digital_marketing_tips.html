FIELD NOTE / DIGITAL MARKETING

Demand first.
Discount second.

Marketing should make the hotel more desirable and easier to find. A promotion is only one tool - and often the most expensive one to overuse.

DISCOVERYBe visible where demand starts.

Search, maps, destination content, social and referral traffic.

CONSIDERATIONGive the guest a reason to choose this hotel.

Photography, reviews, room clarity, location and value proposition.

CONVERSIONMake the direct path competitive.

Rate, flexibility, inclusions, booking-engine usability and trust.

RETURNDo not pay to reacquire every guest.

Guest data, email, loyalty logic and repeat-booking value.

Promotion test

Ask what the discount bought.

GROSS ROOM REVENUE-DISCOUNT-COMMISSION-MEDIA / PAYMENT COST=NET CONTRIBUTION

A campaign can grow bookings and still weaken the economics. Judge promotion depth against incremental demand and net ADR, not the booking count alone.
