Digital marketing guide

Promote with a reason.
Not from habit.

I look at hotel marketing through the same lens as revenue management: what demand problem are we solving, what does it cost and what is left after discount and acquisition cost?

01

Create demand vs capture demand

Google and OTAs are excellent at catching people already looking. Content, partnerships, events and campaigns can create or redirect demand. Know which job each channel is doing.

02

Give every promotion a job

A promotion should answer a specific need period, audience or booking behaviour. If it runs permanently, it has probably become the real price.

Useful promotion

Defined dates, clear target, measurable objective.

Discount addiction

Always-on offer with no test of what demand it actually created.

03

Make direct worth choosing

Direct does not have to mean cheapest. Better flexibility, clearer room choice, useful add-ons and confidence in dealing directly with the hotel can be stronger than another percentage discount.

04

Make content answer travel intent

Talk about why people come, what is nearby, how to get there and what the stay feels like. Useful local content can support both discovery and conversion.

05

Judge net contribution

€10,000 from one channel and €10,000 from another are not necessarily equal. Compare discount, commission, media cost, payment cost, cancellation behaviour and lead time.

  • Net ADR
  • Cost of acquisition
  • Cancellation rate
  • Lead time
  • Incremental demand
