Marketing field guide

Create demand.
Do not sell the discount.

Marketing should solve a commercial problem: create awareness, capture intent, support a need period, strengthen direct demand or improve the value of an existing booking.

Campaign architecture

Give every activity a job.

Create demand

Content, local partnerships, destination positioning and campaigns that give guests a reason to consider the property.

Capture demand

Search, metasearch, OTA visibility and direct-booking paths that intercept guests already looking.

Protect price

Promotions need a target audience, a need period and an end date. Permanent discounting eventually becomes the public price.

Measure net contribution

Headline revenue hides commission, media cost, payment fee, cancellation and the discount itself. The useful question is what demand the campaign added and what that demand was worth after acquisition cost.
