5 practical prioritiesUse this as a briefing checklist with your team or supplier.Ask Plutus a question
01

Separate demand capture from demand creation

OTAs and search capture existing intent. Content, partnerships and campaigns can create or redirect demand.

  • Define the role of each channel
  • Match spend to the booking window
  • Measure incremental value, not only last-click revenue
02

Protect price integrity

A promotion should solve a specific demand problem rather than become the normal public price.

  • Use start and end dates
  • Target need periods and audiences
  • Measure net ADR after commission and discount
03

Build useful direct-booking reasons

Direct benefits should reduce friction or add value without creating an expensive discount race.

  • Flexible direct terms
  • Useful add-ons or room choice
  • Clear service and contact advantages
04

Connect content to demand

Create content around the reasons guests travel, the location and the questions that block conversion.

  • Local events and practical guides
  • Room and experience stories
  • Search intent rather than generic posting
05

Measure net contribution

Revenue alone can hide acquisition cost, commission, cancellation and operational impact.

  • Compare net ADR by channel
  • Include media and payment costs
  • Review cancellation and lead time

The commercial test

Does this make the property easier to understand and easier to book?

That question keeps creative and marketing decisions connected to the guest journey and the hotel's revenue objective.
