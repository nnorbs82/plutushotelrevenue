01 / Demand

Separate demand creation from demand capture.

OTAs and paid search are excellent at capturing existing intent. Content, partnerships, events and targeted campaigns can create or redirect demand. The commercial question is whether you know which job each channel is doing.

Create demand

Local-event content, partnerships, targeted campaigns, packages and reasons to travel.

Capture demand

Brand search, metasearch, OTAs and retargeting users who already intend to book.

02 / Promotions

A promotion should have a problem to solve.

If there is no defined need period, audience or commercial objective, the promotion risks becoming the normal public rate.

  • Define start and end dates.
  • Target specific need periods or audiences.
  • Measure ADR after the discount.
  • Include OTA commission and merchant cost.
  • Review whether the demand was incremental or simply cheaper.

03 / Direct booking

Give guests a reason to book direct that is not simply “cheaper”.

Useful direct benefits can improve conversion without creating a permanent price race.

Useful value

Flexible terms, room preference, useful add-ons, clearer service and easier communication.

Expensive habit

Always discounting the direct rate without understanding the cost or impact on positioning.

04 / Content

Write about why people travel, not just what the hotel wants to announce.

Useful hotel content answers real search intent and removes booking friction.

  • Local events and seasonal travel reasons.
  • Transport, parking and neighbourhood guidance.
  • Room-category differences.
  • Family, business or event-specific travel questions.
  • Content that supports a campaign or need period.

05 / Measurement

Headline revenue can hide an expensive booking.

Compare marketing and distribution through net contribution wherever possible.

  • Room revenue after discount.
  • OTA or affiliate commission.
  • Paid media spend.
  • Merchant/payment costs.
  • Cancellation and no-show behaviour.
  • Length of stay and lead time.
