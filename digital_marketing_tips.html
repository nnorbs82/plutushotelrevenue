Hotel marketing

Digital marketing for hotels: focus on profitable demand

The goal is not to win the most clicks. It is to attract bookings that make commercial sense after commission, media spend and discounts.

Start with the booking economics

Compare the acquisition cost of direct bookings with OTA commission and promotional costs. A direct booking is not automatically more profitable if paid media, discounts and technology costs are ignored.

Protect rate logic

Stacking promotions can train guests to wait for discounts and can weaken your public rate architecture. Every campaign should have a clear demand problem it is intended to solve.

Match campaigns to demand periods

Use marketing when it can realistically influence the booking window. High-demand dates may need less paid support, while soft periods may benefit from focused campaigns or value-led packages.

Measure by segment and channel

Track revenue, cancellation behaviour, length of stay, net ADR and acquisition cost. Marketing performance is much easier to judge when it is connected to the same metrics used in revenue management.
