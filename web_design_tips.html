Hotel web design

Design around the booking decision.

A hotel website is not a digital brochure. It should help the right guest understand the stay, trust the offer and reach the booking engine with minimal friction.

5 practical prioritiesUse this as a briefing checklist with your team or supplier.Ask Plutus a question
01

Lead with a clear promise

The first screen should explain what the property is, where it is and why a guest should choose it.

  • Use a specific headline
  • Show the strongest truthful visual
  • Keep the booking action obvious
02

Structure around guest questions

Organise content by the decisions guests make rather than by internal hotel departments.

  • Rooms and differences
  • Location and transport
  • Facilities, policies and practical details
03

Make rooms easy to compare

Category pages should show layout, bed setup, occupancy, bathroom and important limitations.

  • Avoid vague room names alone
  • Use consistent information blocks
  • Show exact photos for each category
04

Reduce booking friction

Keep the path to availability short and make rate conditions easy to understand.

  • Persistent booking action
  • Fast mobile performance
  • Clear cancellation and inclusion labels
05

Measure meaningful actions

Track booking-engine entries, date searches and completed bookings rather than page views alone.

  • Set conversion events
  • Review mobile and source performance
  • Test one meaningful change at a time

The commercial test

Does this make the property easier to understand and easier to book?

That question keeps creative and marketing decisions connected to the guest journey and the hotel's revenue objective.
