Hotel website design

Design around the booking decision.

I look at a hotel website as part of the commercial journey. It should make the property easy to understand, make room choices easy to compare and move the right guest into the booking engine without unnecessary friction.

01

Make the first screen explain the hotel

Within a few seconds the visitor should understand what the property is, where it is and why it is worth considering. Specific language is more useful than generic luxury wording.

  • Use one clear headline.
  • Show a strong truthful visual.
  • Keep the booking action obvious.
02

Structure content around guest questions

Guests want to know about rooms, location, facilities, parking, breakfast, check-in and policies. Organise those answers around the guest journey rather than internal hotel departments.

  • Rooms and exact differences.
  • Location, parking and transport.
  • Facilities and practical policies.
03

Make room categories easy to compare

Names such as Standard, Superior and Deluxe do not explain enough. Show why the price changes and what the guest actually receives.

  • Use consistent information for size, beds, occupancy and bathroom.
  • Use exact photos for the exact category.
  • Surface the strongest differentiator early.
Better

A repeatable room-information pattern across every category.

Less useful

Important room differences hidden inside long paragraphs.

04

Reduce booking friction

Especially on mobile, a guest should never have to search for how to check dates or what a rate includes.

  • Keep the booking action consistently visible.
  • Use a fast mobile booking engine.
  • Make cancellation terms and inclusions easy to scan.
05

Measure actions that matter

Page views alone do not tell me whether the site is working commercially. I would track room-page visits, booking-engine entries, date searches and completed bookings.

  • Compare mobile and desktop conversion.
  • Compare traffic sources by booking value.
  • Test one meaningful change at a time.

Hotel website checklist

A quick commercial review before worrying about design awards.

✓The first screen clearly explains the hotel.
✓The booking action is easy to find.
✓Room categories can be compared quickly.
✓Room photos match the exact category.
✓Practical guest questions are answered.
✓The mobile journey is fast and uncluttered.
✓Rate conditions are understandable.
✓Booking conversion is measured.
