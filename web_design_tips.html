Hotel website

Hotel web design that supports direct bookings

A hotel website should make the decision to book feel simpler than the OTA journey - especially on a phone.

Put the booking path where guests expect it

A visible booking action should remain easy to find without overwhelming the page. On mobile, test the full journey from landing page to confirmed reservation, not only the homepage.

Explain room categories clearly

Room names, occupancy, bed setup and meaningful differences should be obvious. A guest comparing two room types should not need to open six tabs or decode internal hotel terminology.

Use evidence before adjectives

Specific information is more useful than generic luxury language. Show room size, location, practical amenities, cancellation terms and real photographs. Clear facts reduce uncertainty.

Design for speed and confidence

Compress images, avoid unnecessary scripts, make contact details easy to reach and keep rates consistent with the booking engine. A beautiful homepage cannot compensate for a slow or confusing booking step.
