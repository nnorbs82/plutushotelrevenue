FIELD NOTE 02 / WEBSITE

The website has
one job: reduce doubt.

A hotel website should help a guest understand the property, compare the right room and reach the booking engine without friction.

hotel.com
Stay clear.BOOK A ROOM
THE GUEST DECISION
01Where is it?

Location and context must be immediate.

02What is the hotel like?

Photography and proposition answer this faster than paragraphs.

03Which room fits me?

Room categories need distinct names, features and photographs.

04What is included?

Breakfast, parking, pool, policies and extras should not be hidden.

05Can I book easily?

The booking engine transition should feel like the same journey.

HOMEIs the booking button visible?
ROOMSCan two categories be compared in 20 seconds?
BOOKING ENGINEDoes the rate still make sense after fees, inclusions and conditions?
MOBILECan the full journey be completed one-handed?

Measure the website as a commercial channel.

Traffic is interesting.
Conversion is useful.

Watch direct conversion, booking-engine abandonment, mobile performance, source mix and the net value of a direct booking.
