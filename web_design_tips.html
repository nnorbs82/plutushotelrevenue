01 / NOTICE

Earn attention without slowing the page down.

The first job is not to explain everything. It is to make the hotel legible and desirable quickly enough that the guest keeps exploring.

Mobile UX

Navigation, room discovery and the Book action should work naturally with one hand on a real phone.

Visual identity

Photography, typography and motion should make the property recognisable without hiding what is actually being sold.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Make the product easy to compare.

A guest should not have to decode what separates Standard from Deluxe, or hunt through multiple pages to understand breakfast, parking, room size or location.

Product presentation

Room pages should explain space, bed setup, bathroom, view and category differences with the images beside the facts.

Local discovery

Destination, transport, attractions and event content should answer questions real guests actually have.

03 / TRUST

Remove doubt at the moment it appears.

Trust is not a badge wall. It comes from clear policies, relevant reviews, predictable pricing and a website that behaves consistently.

Social proof

Room feedback belongs near rooms. Breakfast feedback belongs near breakfast. Proof works best beside the decision it supports.

Security + clarity

HTTPS, privacy choices, cancellation rules, payment expectations and real contact information should be easy to find before commitment.

04 / BOOK

Reduce friction all the way through confirmation.

The booking engine is part of the hotel website even when another vendor supplies it. The transition should feel continuous, the rate plans should be understandable and direct value should be concrete.

Booking path

Count the taps from a room page to confirmation. Every unnecessary step is another exit point.

Living technology

AI support, virtual tours and automation are useful when they reduce friction. The best technology is usually the technology the guest barely notices.

THE DESIGN TEST
