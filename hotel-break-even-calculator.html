The simple logic

What counts as fixed and variable?

Fixed costs are costs you carry even with very low occupancy - base payroll, rent, insurance, software, minimum utility consumption and similar overhead.

Variable cost per occupied room is the additional cost created when one more room is occupied - housekeeping, laundry, guest amenities, included breakfast cost and the occupancy-related part of utilities.

If you want a quick management estimate, those two totals are enough. The advanced section only exists for hotels that want to include OTA and payment costs in the contribution calculation.
