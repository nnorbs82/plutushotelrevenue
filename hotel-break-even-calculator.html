How it works

The logic behind the result.

Break-even is reached when the contribution generated by occupied rooms covers the hotel's fixed costs.

1

Available room nights

(Total rooms - rooms out of order) × days in period

2

Contribution per occupied room

Room and other revenue - occupied-room costs - weighted distribution and payment costs

3

Break-even occupancy

Fixed costs ÷ contribution per room ÷ available room nights

Calculator questions

Classify costs carefully.

The biggest errors usually come from mixing fixed and variable costs or entering gross revenue with tax included.

Where should electricity, gas and water go?

Split them. Base consumption that exists regardless of occupancy is fixed. Additional consumption caused by occupied rooms is variable per occupied room.

What is the difference between fixed payroll and housekeeping labour?

Minimum staffing and salaried positions belong in fixed payroll. Extra hourly labour that rises directly with occupied rooms belongs in variable cost.

Should ADR include VAT or sales tax?

No. Use net revenue and net costs on the same basis. Tax collected on behalf of authorities is not hotel revenue.

Does break-even occupancy mean the hotel is healthy?

Not necessarily. Break-even only means the entered costs are covered. Hotels also need capital expenditure, debt obligations, owner returns and resilience for weaker periods.
