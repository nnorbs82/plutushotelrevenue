What goes where?

Keep the first calculation simple.

Fixed costs exist even when occupancy is very low: base payroll, rent, base utilities, insurance, software, maintenance contracts and similar overhead.

Variable cost per occupied room is the extra cost created when one more room is occupied: housekeeping, linen, amenities, included breakfast cost and the occupancy-related part of water, electricity and heating.

For a quick management estimate, those five core inputs are enough. Open the optional distribution section only when you want OTA commissions and payment costs reflected in the contribution.
