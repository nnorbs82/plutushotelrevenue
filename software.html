HOW I EVALUATE HOTEL SOFTWARE

Start with the workflow. Then look at the technology.

01 / Operational fit

How well does the system match reservations, housekeeping, groups, room moves, payments and the way the property actually works?

02 / Commercial control

Can rate products, availability, restrictions and room categories be managed clearly without introducing unnecessary complexity?

03 / Data & reporting

Can the hotel get trustworthy information out of the system quickly enough to use it for revenue, finance and operational decisions?

04 / Ecosystem

Which integrations are genuinely important, which can be native, and what happens to the workflow when one connection fails?

ONE IMPORTANT PRINCIPLE

The newest platform is not automatically the right platform.
