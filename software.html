Evaluation framework

Seven questions before you sign.

A demo should answer operational reality, not just show the cleanest workflow.

01

Property fit

Does it suit your size, room structure, services and operating model?

02

Daily usability

Can reception and reservations complete frequent tasks quickly?

03

Commercial control

Are rate plans, restrictions, reporting and inventory flexible enough?

04

Integrations

Do essential systems connect reliably, and who supports failures?

05

Total cost

Include implementation, payments, modules, support and growth.

06

Data access

Can the hotel export complete useful data without friction?

07

Migration risk

What training, downtime, mapping and historical data work is required?
