Built from hotel operations

Commercial thinking with a practical hotel mindset.

Plutus was founded to make experienced revenue management more accessible to independent and medium-sized accommodation businesses.

My work has moved through hotel operations, reservations and cluster revenue management across Canada, Ireland and Spain. A pricing decision never exists in isolation - it has to work for reception, reservations, sales, finance and the guest.

The approach is deliberately straightforward: understand the hotel's reality, identify what will move performance, explain the reasoning and keep improving.

“The best commercial strategy is one the hotel team can understand, trust and use.”
