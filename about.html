How I work

Simple, informed and transparent.

I want a hotel to understand the commercial logic behind a decision. That means no hiding behind jargon, no recommendation simply because a competitor did it, and no report that ends without a next action.

I work with the numbers closely, but I also keep the guest proposition, the product, the hotel team and the cost of distribution in view. The best revenue strategy is the one the property can actually use.
