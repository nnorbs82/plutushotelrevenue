01 / OPERATIONS

I started close to the guest.

Working in front office and hotel operations taught me what a commercial decision looks like after it leaves the spreadsheet. Room allocation, upgrades, guest expectations, overselling risk, group arrivals and category differences all affect how a hotel can actually sell.

That background still shapes the way I work. A revenue strategy is only useful when the hotel can execute it cleanly.

02 / REVENUE

Then the numbers became the daily language.

Pricing, pickup, pace, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, booking window, business mix and forecast became the framework for making decisions. But the numbers always stay connected to the property and the guest proposition.

I prefer commentary that ends with an action. A metric matters when it changes what the hotel does next.

03 / SYSTEMS

Revenue lives inside the technology stack.

PMS configuration, channel managers, OTAs, booking engines, rate products, payment flows and reporting all influence the commercial result. A sensible pricing strategy can still fail if the systems cannot execute it properly.

That is why I treat hotel technology and distribution as part of revenue management rather than as somebody else's technical problem.

04 / PLUTUS

Independent means direct.

Plutus lets me work directly with owners and hotel teams without an account layer in between. The person discussing the strategy is the same person checking pickup, questioning the forecast and making the recommendation.

I prefer that closeness because it makes communication faster, keeps the reasoning visible and allows the work to grow around the actual hotel rather than a generic service package.
