A working dossier rather than a corporate biography.

Commercial experience before consultancy.

I came to independent consulting through hotel operations and revenue-management work. That matters because pricing decisions rarely live in isolation - they touch reservations, front office, distribution, marketing, group business and owner expectations.

I prefer clarity over complexity.

A sophisticated revenue strategy does not need to feel complicated. The goal is to make the hotel easier to steer: understand demand, know the trade-offs and make the next decision with confidence.

My role stays close to the property.

I do not want Plutus to become a distant reporting service. I want to understand the hotel's room categories, booking patterns, local calendar, channel mix, quirks and operational reality.
