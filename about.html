About Plutus

Simple thinking.
Serious hotel experience.

Plutus was built around a straightforward idea: independent and medium-sized hotels should be able to access experienced revenue management without needing to build a large commercial department.

Founded 2020 Barcelona Hotel-first approach

Norbert Nica

Founder - Plutus Hotel Revenue

Connect on LinkedIn ↗

Revenue management should make a hotel easier to run - not add another layer of jargon, dashboards and meetings.

Plutus provides bespoke outsourced revenue management for hotels of different sizes and markets. The focus is on useful commercial action: pricing, forecasting, business mix, distribution, group strategy and clear communication with the people running the property every day.

The mission remains the same as when Plutus started: offer affordable, straightforward revenue management and help hotels improve room revenue as much as possible while keeping the relationship personal.
