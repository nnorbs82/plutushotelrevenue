Data controller

Plutus Hotel Revenue Management, based in Barcelona, Spain, is responsible for enquiries received through this website.

Information submitted

If you use the contact form, Plutus receives the information you choose to provide, such as your name, hotel or company, email address and message. The break-even calculator runs in your browser and does not send your financial assumptions to Plutus.

Why it is used

Contact information is used to receive, assess and respond to enquiries and, where relevant, to take steps requested before entering a service agreement.

Service providers

The contact form uses FormSubmit to relay submissions to the Plutus email inbox. External providers operate under their own privacy terms. Do not submit confidential hotel financial information through the contact form.

Retention

Business enquiries are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a relationship, meet legal obligations or establish or defend legal claims.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, or object to certain processing. You may also contact the competent data-protection authority.

Cookies and local storage

The core site does not intentionally set advertising cookies. The calculator does not persist hotel inputs in browser storage in this version.
