This notice explains how Plutus Hotel Revenue Management handles personal data submitted through this website. It should be reviewed whenever website services or suppliers change.

1. Data controller

Plutus Hotel Revenue Management, based in Barcelona, Spain, is the controller for enquiries received through this website. Privacy requests can be sent to norbert.nica@plutushotelrevenue.com.

2. Data collected

When you use the contact form, Plutus collects the information you choose to provide, such as your name, hotel or company, email address and message. The website does not require an account. The break-even calculator runs in your browser and Plutus does not receive the financial assumptions you enter.

3. Why the information is processed

Contact details are used to receive, assess and respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to take steps requested before entering a service agreement. Depending on the enquiry, processing is based on pre-contractual steps or legitimate interests in managing business communications.

4. Service providers and transfers

The contact form uses FormSubmit to relay submissions to the Plutus email inbox. Form information is processed by that provider for delivery and may be handled outside the European Economic Area. Do not submit confidential hotel financial information through the contact form. External services have their own privacy notices.

5. Retention

Business enquiries are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage the relationship, establish or defend legal claims and meet legal obligations. Enquiries that do not lead to an ongoing relationship are periodically deleted.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to certain processing. You may also complain to the competent data-protection authority. Identity may need to be verified before completing a request.

7. Cookies and local storage

The core website does not intentionally set advertising cookies. The break-even calculator runs entirely in your browser. The V5 calculator does not save the figures you enter after you leave or reload the page. Hosted fonts and form delivery may receive standard technical request information.

8. Changes

This notice may be updated when the website, suppliers or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current version.

Important

This operational website notice is not a substitute for legal advice and should be reviewed before the redesigned branch goes into production.
