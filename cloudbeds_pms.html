My revenue-management view

01

For an independent hotel, one connected platform can be valuable when it genuinely reduces duplicate work between PMS, channel management, booking engine and reporting.

02

A wide platform is only an advantage if the property will use the connected parts. I would test the calendar, rate workflow and reporting with real hotel scenarios rather than a demo script.

03

Before migration, every distribution connection, critical report, rate-plan rule and operational dependency should be mapped. The commercial workflow matters as much as the product breadth.
