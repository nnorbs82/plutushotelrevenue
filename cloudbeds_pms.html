My revenue-management view

The appeal is the breadth of one platform.

For independent hotels, Cloudbeds can be attractive when the goal is to reduce fragmentation across PMS, distribution, booking engine, guest marketing and revenue tools.

The evaluation should still start with the property’s actual workflows. A wide platform is valuable only when the hotel will use the connected parts and the configuration fits the way it sells rooms.

  • Test the calendar and rate-management workflow with real scenarios.
  • Map every distribution connection before migration.
  • Check reporting depth against your current commercial reporting.
  • Understand which capabilities are included and which are separate products.
