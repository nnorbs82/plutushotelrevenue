THE UNIFIED STACK

Five layers.
Open one.

Hover a layer to expand it. The question is not whether Cloudbeds has many modules - it is whether those layers work deeply enough together to simplify the hotel.

01 / FOUNDATION

Operations

The operational base covers reservations, calendar, room status and housekeeping. This is the layer every later commercial function depends on.

  • Reservations and room inventory
  • Calendar and room-status workflows
  • Housekeeping and daily operations
  • Rate plans and availability controls
02 / REACH

Distribution

Cloudbeds cites a broad channel ecosystem. For a revenue team, connectivity only matters when mappings, restrictions and inventory controls remain understandable.

  • 300+ distribution channels cited by Cloudbeds
  • Rate and inventory synchronisation
  • Channel mapping and restrictions
  • Distribution without per-channel maintenance
03 / OWNED DEMAND

Direct

Booking-engine, website and marketing capabilities push the platform into the hotel's own acquisition journey rather than leaving direct demand outside the PMS stack.

  • Booking engine and direct conversion
  • Website and digital-marketing tools
  • Immersive booking experience
  • Guest relationship and repeat demand
04 / COMMERCIAL

Revenue

Pricing and commercial controls sit increasingly close to operations. Revenue teams should test whether native controls and reporting are deep enough for the property.

  • Pricing and revenue workflows
  • Business-mix and performance visibility
  • Group and event sales expansion
  • Bookable non-room Spaces
05 / INTELLIGENCE

Data

Data Insights, accounting infrastructure and Ask Signals move Cloudbeds toward a broader management layer over the operating record.

  • Data Insights and reporting
  • Cloudbeds Accounting
  • Ask Signals conversational intelligence
  • Operational + commercial data in one environment
2026 / WHAT CHANGED

The edges of the platform moved outward.

The newest releases matter because they move Cloudbeds beyond a conventional independent-hotel PMS into sales, spaces, accounting, intelligence and marketing.

GROUPS & EVENTS

Group sales inside the platform

Newer workflows extend beyond transient room reservations into organised group business.

SPACES

Inventory beyond bedrooms

Bookable spaces expand the commercial model for meeting areas and other non-room products.

DIRECT

Immersive booking engine

The booking experience has been pushed toward stronger product presentation and direct conversion.

INTELLIGENCE

Data Insights + Ask Signals

Reporting and conversational access to the platform's data layer are becoming part of the proposition.

FINANCE

Accounting infrastructure

Accounting and invoicing work aim to reduce the distance between operational data and financial reporting.

MARKETING

Digital demand tools

OTA Ads and review-response tooling bring more acquisition work into the wider ecosystem.

MY HOTEL FIT TEST

Cloudbeds makes sense when consolidation removes real work.

STRONG FIT

Independent hotels wanting one connected environment

Test the combined PMS, distribution and direct-booking experience rather than each module in isolation.

WATCH CLOSELY

Commercial and group complexity

Use real room types, restrictions, groups, events and reporting requirements in the demonstration.

COMMERCIAL TEST

What duplicate work disappears?

If specialist tools remain necessary, the value of consolidation should be measured against what the platform truly replaces.
